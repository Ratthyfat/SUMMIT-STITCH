Our story

Two of us, a pair of needles, and the Front Range

Summit & Stitch started at our kitchen table — one of us knitting, the other keeping the coffee going. We make things slowly, on purpose.

Every piece is knit by hand in Colorado Springs, one skein at a time. No factory, no season-chasing. We'd rather make ten pieces that last a decade than a hundred that don't.

We believe things worth having are worth waiting for, and worth keeping — that belief runs deeper than knitwear for us.
