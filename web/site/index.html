Our story

Two of us, a pair of needles, and the Front Range

Summit & Stitch started at our kitchen table — one of us knitting, the other keeping the coffee going. We make things slowly, on purpose, with heirloom intent: buy it once, wear it long, hand it down.

That belief runs deeper than knitwear for us. It's how we try to live, how we work, and why we'd rather do this small and right than big and fast.
